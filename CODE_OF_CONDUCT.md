# Code of Conduct

VHScribe is a practical preservation project. We want its community to be welcoming to beginners,
archivists, media enthusiasts, and experienced Linux contributors alike.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Make technical claims honestly and distinguish tests from assumptions.
- Respect privacy: do not solicit or repost personal recordings or configuration.
- Accept maintainers' decisions about project scope and safety.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate exposure of private data,
and sustained disruptive behavior are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer contact options on the GitHub profile. Do
not include sensitive material in a public issue. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions,
temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participants when needed to protect the
community. Reports will be handled as confidentially and fairly as practical.

This policy applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, and project-related public
spaces where someone is representing VHScribe.
